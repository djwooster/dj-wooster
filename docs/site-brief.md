# djwooster.com — Site Brief

Oct 26, 2026 · @DJ Wooster

The homepage exists to turn warm leads into "Start a project" inquiries for custom websites and apps from $15K. Everything else is secondary or lives elsewhere.

## Primary audience

Established business owners with real revenue who need a custom website, web app or iOS app, and understand what software is worth to their business.

- **How they arrive:** DJ met them in person, at networking events or out in the world, and sent them the link. About 1–2 new leads a week.
- **What they already know:** DJ's name and face, and usually the project they want to discuss.
- **What they need from the site:** confirmation that DJ is serious and his work is excellent, and an obvious next step.
- **Not the homepage audience:** course buyers, vibe coders wanting audits, and cold traffic from ads. They get their own pages and funnels.

## Primary goal and conversion

The single goal is a qualified project inquiry. The single primary CTA, everywhere on the site, is a button labeled **Start a project**.

- It appears in the header (always visible), the hero, after the work section, and as the closing section.
- It opens a short form at `/start`, not a calendar:
  1. Name and email
  2. Company and website
  3. What you need: Website · Web app · iOS app · Not sure yet
  4. Timeline: ASAP · 1–3 months · 3+ months
  5. Budget: $15–25K · $25–50K · $50K+
  6. A few sentences about the project
- The confirmation reads: "Thanks. I'll reply within one business day with a link to book a call."
- State **Projects from $15K** near the CTA so leads arrive pre-qualified. No budget option below $15K.
- Every submission records its source (homepage, work page, a specific case study).

## Secondary goals, ranked

1. **Prove DJ ships real, working products.** Live products with live status do the convincing.
2. **Explain why custom work can be fast.** A short process section; AI is the reason behind the speed, not the headline.
3. **Get discovered.** The blog, structured data and `llms.txt` stay in place for search and LLMs.
4. **Feed the course funnel.** *Ship Your App with Claude Code* gets its own ad-driven landing pages; the homepage only links to it from the footer.
5. **Route to YouTube and Skool.** Footer links only.

## Core message

The direction: fully custom work, delivered faster than the buyer expects, with no drop in quality. Most providers offer two of custom, fast and excellent; DJ claims all three, and the live products prove it.

| Option | Headline (two-tone: ink, then gray) | Subline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A (lead)** | Custom websites and apps. *Delivered fast.* | Designed and built around your business by one senior designer-developer. Live in weeks, not months. |
| B | Custom software, *on today's timeline.* | Websites, web apps and iOS apps built for how your business actually works, shipped in weeks. |
| C | Built for your business. *Shipped in weeks.* | Custom websites and apps, designed and engineered end to end, without the agency timeline. |

**Supporting line for the process section:** "I design and build with modern AI tools, so you get fully custom work in weeks instead of months, without cutting corners."

## What the hero shows

The hero shows live products on real devices, because the belief a visitor must leave with in five seconds is: **he ships real, working products.** Showing beats telling.

- An iPhone frame running FocusMode, beside a browser frame showing the RE-IQ dashboard. Add the art gallery site once the client approves.
- A small mono status chip on each: `LIVE` · `IN APP STORE REVIEW` (switch to `ON THE APP STORE` at approval).
- Subtle, production-grade motion: real UI states cycling inside the frames (the timer counting, a dashboard row updating). No abstract decoration.
- Headline, subline and **Start a project** on the left; devices on the right. On mobile, the text comes first and the devices sit below.
- The line **Projects from $15K** sits under the CTA in small mono.

## Homepage section order

1. **Hero:** state the offer and prove it on sight with live products; *Start a project*.
2. **Selected work:** FocusMode, RE-IQ, SourceIQ and client sites as case-study cards (problem, what was built, status, link). Proves range and craft.
3. **What I build:** three services: websites, custom web apps, iOS apps (React Native). Lets the visitor find their project.
4. **How it works:** four numbered steps (call, scope, build, launch) with typical timelines. Explains the speed and removes the fear of a big investment.
5. **Why one person:** design and engineering in one head, no handoffs, owner-level thinking from someone who runs his own products. Answers "why not an agency?"
6. **Start a project:** a closing block with the CTA and "Projects from $15K."
7. **Footer:** Writing, YouTube, Skool, the course, contact, social links.

**Header nav:** Work · Services · Process · About, plus the **Start a project** button. Writing joins the nav once it has three real posts.

## Cut or deferred

- **"Join the waitlist" as a homepage CTA:** removed. The course waitlist lives on the course's own funnel pages.
- **Course promotion on the homepage:** footer link only. *Ship Your App with Claude Code* gets ad-driven landing pages with the full flow.
- **Business-idea landing pages:** live at unlisted URLs, excluded from the nav and sitemap, reached only through ads.
- **Audits for vibe coders:** deferred.
- **Claude Code for UX Designers:** deferred.
- **Newsletter as a primary goal:** dropped from the homepage; email capture stays on course and blog pages.
- **The joint venture:** kept off the site until it is live.
- **Blog in the main nav:** waits for three real posts; the scaffolding, structured data and `llms.txt` stay.
- **Testimonials and client logos:** only real ones, added as they come. No placeholders.

## Success metrics: first 90 days

The site is working if warm leads turn into signed projects at $15K and up.

| Metric | 90-day target |
| --- | --- |
| Leads sent to the site | 15–20 |
| "Start a project" submissions | 30%+ of leads |
| Calls held | 8–10 |
| Projects signed | 2–3, each $15K+ ($30–45K+ total) |
| Leads who mention specific work on the call | Most of them |

- **Capacity assumption:** about 15 hours a week of client work, one build at a time. Adjust the signed-project target if that changes.
- **Tracking:** form submissions with source, CTA clicks per section, and a simple lead log (date met, sent link, submitted, call, signed).
